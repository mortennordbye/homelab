**Inputs:** Your slide text (titles and bullets), pasted in place of `{{TEXT}}`.
**Expected output:** Only the revised slide text, in the same structure — no preamble, quotes, or commentary.

---

Act as an expert presentation copyeditor. Fix the grammar, spelling, and punctuation of the slide text below, and tighten it for a PowerPoint slide.
