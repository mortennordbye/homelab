The fix gets written down properly, with the manifests that are running, because the whole thing is public in the [Homelab repository](https://github.com/mortennordbye/Homelab).

Most posts cover [Kubernetes](/tags/kubernetes/), [observability](/tags/observability/), [GitOps](/tags/gitops/) and [Azure](/tags/azure/). How the cluster itself is built lives on [docs.nordbye.it](https://docs.nordbye.it/), and who writes this is on the [about page](/about/).
